## Sales Report :

- **Project objective:** 

    **1.** Create a _[customer performance report](https://github.com/mshubhamdora22/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)_ 

    **2.** Conduct a comprehensive comparison between _[market performance and sales targets](https://github.com/mshubhamdora22/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target%20Report.pdf)_

- **Purpose of sales analytics:** Empower businesses to monitor and evaluate their sales activities and performance.

- **Importance of analyzing sales data:** Identify sales patterns and track key performance indicators (KPIs).

- **Role of reports:** Determine effective customer discounts, facilitate negotiations with consumers, and identify potential business expansion opportunities in promising countries.
- **Key Insights**
- 1. The sales data indicates a substantial increase in total revenue, showing a 304.5% growth from 2020 to 2021. Amazon emerged as the leading customer in 2021 with sales totaling 82.1 million, and widespread growth is evident across multiple key accounts.
- 2. The market performance report highlights that most countries fell short of their targets for 2021, resulting in an overall shortfall of 9.17% against the combined targets. While sales showed growth, the gap indicates that sales strategies in key markets did not fully align with the aggressive targets set for the year.
- **Recommendations** Focus on strengthening relationships with top-performing customers like Amazon to maximize revenue, and evaluate and refine sales stategies in regions that missed their targets to improve alignment with future goals. 
-**Future Enhancements** Consider integrating predictive analytics to forecast future sales and developing more interactive dashboard features to enhance data exploration. 

## Finance Report :

- **Project objective:** 

    **1.** Create Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by _[Fiscal Year](https://github.com/mshubhamdora22/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Fiscal%20Year.pdf)_ & _[Months](https://github.com/mshubhamdora22/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Months.pdf)_ 

   **2.** Create Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by _[Markets](https://github.com/mshubhamdora22/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Markets.pdf)_

- **Purpose of sales analytics:** Evaluation of financial performance, support decision-making, and facilitate communication with stakeholders.

- **Importance of analyzing Finance data:** Aid in benchmarking against industry peers and previous periods Foundation for budgeting and forecasting.

- **Role of reports:** Align financial planning with strategic goals Instill confidence in the organization's financial outlook.
- **Key Insights**
- 1. The Profit & loss statement by fiscal year shows strong, consistent growth in net sales, which increased from 87.5 million to nearly 600 million by 2021. Despite this revenue expansion, the gross margin percentage experienced a slight decline, dropping from 41.4% to 36.4%.
- 2. The monthly analysis reveals clear seasonality, with consistent sales performance variations across quarters and recurring sales declines in the third quarter, notably in March, before rebounding toward the fourth quarter.
- 3. The Profit & Loss statement by markets for FY 2021 highlights India as the primary revenue driver, generating the highest net sales of 161.26M, followed by substantial contributions from the USA and South Korea. While top markets drove overall volume, profitability varied widely, with regions like New Zealand and Japan delivering the highest gross margins.
- **Recommendations**
- 1. Focus cost-reduction and pricing strategies on high-revenue markets like India and the USA to stabilize declining gross margins while maintaining market share.
- 2. Implement targeted marketing campaigns and early discounting programs to flaten the recurring sales slump observed Q3, specifically around March.
- 3. Replicate the operational and product-mix models of top-margin regions like New Zealand and Japan across underperforming Eurpean Makrets like Germany.
- **Future Enhancements**
- 1. Integrate time-series predictive modeling to anticipate seasonal dips and dynamically align inventory levels.
- 2. Develop sub-zone and SKU-level contribution margin views within the dashboard to isolate specific cost drivers.
- 3. Add interactive "what-if" parameters allowing stakeholders to test the impact of pricing revisions and COGS shift on net profitability.
